THE IDEA BEHIND THE PROJECT

Why I Created DFOL

DFOL = Dump Folder — Poore Folder Ka Dump.

Before designing DFOL, I sometimes found it difficult to bring a whole server or software project into one readable document. The work is spread across many folders and files, and collecting it all can be slow and frustrating. I experienced this problem myself.

I wanted to give the world a rough design proposal for a simpler workflow: open a folder in a File Manager, click its three-dot menu, choose DFOL, and export the whole folder into one structured TXT document containing its paths, structure and supported file contents.

This is a proposed design, not a claim that the final industry solution is complete. Other developers and companies can improve it, update it and build it at a larger scale. Even in the age of AI and the internet, poor workflows and inadequate tools can make people slower and more frustrated. I want to contribute an idea that helps people work faster.

The aim is simple: make it easier to bring a complete project together in one place, understand it and share it when needed.
